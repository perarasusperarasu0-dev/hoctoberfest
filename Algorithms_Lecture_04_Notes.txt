Topic: Divide-and-Conquer Sorting Algorithms
Professor: Dr. Henderson | Midterm Date: November 12

Key Concepts Covered:
1. MergeSort: Recursively divides array into two halves, sorts each half, and merges them.
   - Time Complexity: O(n log n) in best, average, and worst cases.
   - Space Complexity: O(n) auxiliary space required for merging.

2. QuickSort: Chooses a pivot element and partitions elements smaller/larger than pivot.
   - Average Time Complexity: O(n log n), Worst Case: O(n^2) when poorly chosen pivot.
